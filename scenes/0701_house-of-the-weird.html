House of the Weird

November 20, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 7 Scene 1 draft 720 words

Please leave comments on the scene using the form at the end of the page.

Ryan dropped me off at my father’s, leaving me in the dark to my thoughts. I stood on the porch and stared off towards the hilltops around me, and kind of just, took in being back at home. Both in the literal sense of this being my childhood home, and the sense of the locality. I tried to let the unease slide off of me that I’d felt at the police station, as well as the agony of emotion that had unleashed itself on the ride back from the fire tower, and the worry for Dad that I’d felt on the trail. This had been a prolonged and very tiring day to say the least. I flipped my wrist over, causing the face of my watch to illuminate with the motion. Midnight. I'd been going for 22 hours straight, no wonder I could feel the fraying webs at the corners of my mind, and the ache in my shoulder that said I had overextended. I sighed and turned, opening the door that, at least despite all the bad blood between us right now, Temperance Bennett-Sands|Temperance had left unlocked for me, and saw myself into the living room, where I found not only Temper, sitting on the loveseat and quietly drinking something out of a stout mug, but also Anna Caplan|Anna. "Oh hey guys. I didn’t realize anyone would be up at this point." “Well, I came to update Temp on Abraham, and then she said you were staying and she expected you back at any minute. I guess----” a glint of gold from her wrist flashed as she cast her eyes to it. “—we might have gotten involved in our conversation.” Temperance for her part looked at me coolly over the edge of her mug. She didn’t say anything venomous, so that was progress. “Yes. We got involved and stayed up. Glad to see you came back in one piece.” There was a quick glance at Anna. “Kitchen’s clean. I think we have some sandwich stuff if you want something.” Not warm, but also not poisonous. More progress. “I think I’ll just—,” My knees groaned and my spine cracked under the surface of my skin like a rusted bike chain as I sank into my father’s recliner across from Temperance. “–sit.” A rattle that would challenge the dying escaped from me, a sigh that practically spelled the weariness I felt. “Judah? When’s… the last time you slept? or ate for that matter?” asked Anna, a motherly sternness taking hold in her voice as she eyed me critically. “Dunno. What month is it?” I felt Temperance grunt with a mirthless snort, and heard her get up from her chair. I stole a glance from a half lidded eye and saw Anna looking at her retreating form pointedly. “Thanks Temp” I said tiredly, and leaned my head back on the chair. Temperance grunted, and then hitched. I opened an eye and she looked towards the seam of the ceiling and wall, like she’d heard something. She shook her head and continued into the kitchen. I eyed the seam suspiciously. I guess I could help dad out and check the attic for pests. Later. Right now the recliner was too comfortable and warm. I should get a recliner like this. "Judah, Is there any news?" “Technically I’m not allowed to comment on a pending investigations. However, I can comment that I did not find him while I was up there, and did find evidence that he’d gotten away from the carnage on the trail.” I set my jaw. “And we’ll find him. I just need to find more clues.” Anna smiled “Well, that’s better news than nothing.” and I tried my best to smile back at her, and just passively listened to Temperance clink dishes in the kitchen, and closed my eyes to rest them a second. I thought about that garnet plinth dad had drawn in his journal again. What was he going on about? The clicks and clanks got more distant as I tried to hold on to my train of thought. Missing dads and magic stones. Helluva day. My shoulder throbbed in protest again. The image of the gemstone in my my mind’s eye pulsed with a pinkish light from within it’s core and matched the pang. Wait. Magic stone?
