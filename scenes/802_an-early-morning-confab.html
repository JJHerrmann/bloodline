An Early Morning Confab

November 20, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 8 Scene 2 draft 706 words

Please leave comments on the scene using the form at the end of the page.

Ryan yawned as he sat at the dining room table. “So, let me get this straight.” he folded his hands together and pointed them at me. “You, wake up, see a light, start to go down the hallway. The light can’t possibly be hitting where you saw it.” “For the twenty third time now, yes.” I said flatly, taking a sip from a mug of coffee I’d made after getting Temperance to come off the bed and out into the living room. She was holding her own mug with both hands, knees tucked to her chest, wrapped in a knit throw that’d come from Great-Grandma Sands. “You see the light coming from under Temperance’s bedroom door, You open it, and you see her—” “—floating. Yes.” “With venous discoloration. I can attest to that. it was still visible when I got here.” Anna chimed in. She was standing across the coffee table from Temperance, trying to not directly watch her, but also definitely watching her. Her face was etched in a look of pure concern and absolute no bullshit business. When I’d called her earlier, apologizing profusely for waking her, and tried to sketch the situation she’d almost hung up on me. Then I’d sent her a photo of Temperance’s arm. She’d appeared minutes later and did a battery of at home tests on her, checking her for bruises, pupil dilation responses, anything it seemed like someone trained would think of. “And her eyes were glowing.” Ryan said, looking back at me. “Yep. Green.” “Like, what are we talking about? Like her eyes were just bright? She’s got green eyes.” I looked at him sourly. “No, more green than normal. Like not Nuclear goo green, but like—” “If you shined a flashlight through an emerald.” Temperance whispered, her voice shaking. Anna narrowed her eyes at the meek sound. It was very not Temperance. “And she was whispering.” Ryan said, slowly, concern also etched into his face as he watched Temperance take a small sip from her mug. “Yes. In Welsh.” “Do you know Welsh?” “No. But for some reason I know it was Welsh.” “And she was saying something about binding?” Temperance slowly turned her head to him. “Ryan. It was practically a spell. I don’t remember saying it. I don’t remember any of this, just waking up and finding Judah in my room holding that— thing.” she tilted her mug at the garnet. Sitting in the middle of the coffee table, under the carnival glass candy dish lid. it’s own little quarantine. “Though the blood is not pure, the pact is bound. Awaken O sleeper. The time has come for the healing word. The spirit that crossed your path is now bound.” She shrunk further into the throw. “Whatever the fuck that means.” Ryan paused and considered. “And you said all that in Welsh?” “Apparently I whispered it creepily according to Captain Jackass.” “Temperance,” his tone flat and directive, chiding Temperance for name calling. “Ryan.” I said softly. “It’s the stone that was in the journal. I guess it was in the journal when I tagged it? Maybe fell out into my pocket when I pulled the journal out and discovered the hole?” “That’s evidence?” Ryan asked incredulously. “I mean, yeah.” “Under the candy dish lid.” “I mean, what was I supposed to do after all that, throw it in a ziplock?” “I don’t think anyone should be touching it at all.” Anna said flatly. “I’ve never seen discoloration like that before. Temperance appears to be physically fine, but that’s… horrifying. Even ignoring the other weirdness.” An electronic rooster crowed from her pocket. “That’s my alarm for Abraham. I need to get up to his place and check in on him.” She grabbed her bag from beside the door and looked back at Temperance. “If you—” she looked at me “—either of you, see any changes in the other or yourself as far as physical responses, you let me know immediately. I don’t know what that stone is, or where it came from, but that was more than just an allergic reaction to something and we have to watch.” We gave the obligatory “Yes ma’am.” as she pushed open the door and closed it behind her.
