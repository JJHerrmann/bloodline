The Bear, Officially

November 19, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 6 Scene 1 draft 151 words

We all reconvened on the trail. Beech had his arms crossed in front of him, leaning back against a tree trunk as I'd come to expect from my grandfather. Duilion and Lee thanked us for the time and for getting them up to speed then started down the trail, saying they'd report in and get in touch with Forestry. I glanced at Ryan, who, in contrast to Chief Beech, was straight as a board and had his hands wound into tight fists as we all stood in the trail wrapping up. "For all that brass—" Cooperfield said, his angered flush evaporating, replaced with a smooth, practiced prose analysis tailor-made for reporters "—the simplest answer is that your Pa hired a bunch of well-armed amateurs that couldn't hit the broad side of a—" He caught himself. "Point is, let's not make it more than it is. Tragic, but an animal attack nonetheless."
