The Too-Early Morning After

November 20, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 8 Scene 1 draft 549 words

Please leave comments on the scene using the form at the end of the page.

“Temperance. Are you ok?” I hoped that wasn’t as shaky sounding as I felt. Cause I was really out on a limb here. “Judah? WHY THE FUCK—” She started, her temper launching off. I pointed at her arm, where the blackened veins were still visible. “What?” she rubbed at the arm. “There’s no way I slept through you doing something as childish as tracing my veins—” “Fucking really? I would have just gone to bed over that Temp.” I complained stepping back, backing myself against her bedroom wall and sinking to the floor before my shaking knees betrayed me and I fell on my ass. “What is with you?” She asked, bewildered. She looked at me like I was the one doing the weird thing, and not the one that had just walked in on her unconscious Welsh David Copperfield act. I held up the stone, the pink glow fading but still ember bright in the now near black of the room. “Well, I found this.” “And? I know you found it. It fell out of your pocket when you were asleep in the recliner. I put it on the coffee table next to your sandwich that you’d fallen asleep before you could eat.” “It wasn’t next to the sandwich.” I pointed at the door. “I woke up, ate the sandwich, started to go to the study, and saw a light coming from under your door—” “And you didn’t think “Oh, it’s just a nightlight?”” “Literally my first thought. That you had the creepiest pink oscillating night light that I’d ever seen.” I shook my head ruefully. “But that didn’t explain why I could see it from the recliner, and also you don’t have a pink oscillating night light.” She pursed her lips. She wanted to argue. Not that I could see her in the dark yet, but That’s what I imagined was happening. “Well, I mean—” “Anyway, so I knock, and am like “temperance?” and open the door and find you doing the absolute hardest Gozer cosplay. Floating over the bed, hair everywhere like you sleep in a fish tank, this little fucker,” I gestured with the stone “doing it’s best impression of a glowstick, and your arm veins going black. So I grabbed it from you.” She stared at me and was very still. Ominously still. “Bullshit.” “I’d have thought so had I not literally just had it happen to me.” “Bullshit. You are just trying to freak me out now.” A whisper. “I mean, it’d make it a party of two, cause I’m pretty fucking freaked myself.” I stared at the stone. “you were—” “Don’t. I don’t want to hear any more. Get out of my room Judah.” “Awake O Sleeper.” I said. Not really paying attention to her, trying to get my bearings. Why did I know that’s what she said? “…The time has come for the healing word.” she echoed. Her breathing got shakey and even in the dark I could see her trembling. “The spirit who crossed your path—” “—is now bound.” I finished. “Judah” Her voice was a plea, a tearful plea. “Why did I know that?” I looked down at the stone in my hand, The ember of pink light fading out. “I don’t know Temper, but it can’t be good.”
