Them Sands Boys

November 25, 2025 Act 2 Chapter 12 Scene 3 draft 1745 words

Please leave comments on the scene using the form at the end of the page.

We stared at each other, almost daring the other to throw the first punch, when we were snapped out of our argument by a blaring red light over the door. It didn't take a military genius to figure out that wasn't meant to signal someone's birthday. "Goddamnit, Judah, what did you do?" I looked shocked "Literally walked in the door and was escorted to your office. I didn't even have time to steal anything." Levi hmphed at my attempt to lighten the situation and swung open the door to the exterior hallway, and in front of him was a mountain of a man. Dressed in black fatigue pants and a dark grey button down with more bulk underneath it than one would find strictly needed for security. "What's goi-" Levi started. "You." came the gravel thick growl from the security guard grabbing Levi by the collar. "They are in Quad 1!" Uh-oh. I stepped to the left, around my brother, and kicked the offending guard in the side of his knee with most of the effort I could manage. The kick shifted his leg and destabilized his stance, causing him to yelp in surprise, let go of Levi and nearly topple over sideways. The nice thing about someone that goes to the gym as opposed to something like working on the farm or having a highly demanding physical job that requires a lot of movement is a lack of training on muscle groups designed to keep your muscles in track for anything other than what the machines you are using to bulk up is using them for. This includes things like adductors and knees, which most creatine crunching gym bros refuse to focus on because it doesn't do much for the Instagram photos. Before he could make more of a scene I grabbed his other leg and dragged him into the closet with us. "Shut the door!" I hissed quietly. "What!? Judah, That's Dave, I've worked with him for years-" "And he didn't seem pleased to see you, Levi, if you didn't notice. Now shut the door, there's two of us and--" I punched the guard in the nose trying to get him to quiet down "-- we don't know how many more of them will be this happy to see you either." "ME? I work here!" "He didn't grab for me, and I'm three inches taller. He clearly saw me." I began to investigate the duty belt around Dave's waist. Zip Tie restraints, small knife, pepper spray, no gun but wear on the belt that indicated he did frequently have a holster attached. So guns were more of a “not yet” than “not”. I collected the pepper spray and used one of the zip ties to restrain Dave to the water pipe coming from the brick wall next to the shelves. "Well, what do we do?" "We leave, but we have to be smart and not--" Levi had already opened the door to shouts. Great. I grabbed the back of his belt and yanked, pulling him back in the closet as a pair of tines chewed up the wood of the doorframe. One Taser down I guess. Levi shifted in the corner, eyes popping at Dave being trussed up against one of the wire shelves in the closet. I had an idea. Unfortunately, even with these “safe” pepper spray cans that fire a focused squirt of pepper spray, there is some blowback, and lingering in the air, which means we were going to get as good as we gave with it. "Levi, have you ever been pepper sprayed before?" "Oh because I did drugs I must have got--" I just stared at him. "... Ok fine there was that one time." "Good, so you know what the deal is going to be. Find some liquid and wet your shirt." I started examining the pepper spray can for how I could lodge the trigger into something akin to a fogger. "AND PREFERABLY NOT SOMETHING LIKE BLEACH." I said loudly and pointedly hearing my brother foraging around plastic jugs. I slipped behind the hinge side of the door while Levi was pouring what I hoped wasn't a corrosive chemical on his shirt and popped the handle. Someone took and pushed through, opening the crack at the hinge wide. "Pss." I said as one of the guards made eye contact through the gap and I pushed the button down on the pepper spray, launching a stream of the devil's clear-eyes into his face. "Judah!" I heard and flinched back towards the wall as the door suddenly had two eye holes, like a sad paper bag Halloween mask. Oh shit someone did have their gun. And here I was without mine. Lovely. I jammed my shoulder hard into the door, with as much leverage as I could muster being trapped behind the door and at the wrong end of the lever. The guard I'd pepper sprayed was still yowling like a hamstrung cat, and I heard a panicked oof as someone was misbalanced by the moving slab of wood. Hopefully-- CRACK. Oh good. His pistol was pointed up that time, I thought as dust rained from the ceiling. Jamming the spray can against one of the holes in the door, I unloaded it again, all the more distraction so I could push my way out of my own trap than actually hitting anyone. Which as I slid past the pinch of the door and the shelving unit against the wall without catching a bullet I assumed was working. There were three guards, the one holding his eyes and waving a baton that I had pepper sprayed, the one coughing uncontrollably like he'd had his mouth open when I'd sprayed through the hole, and the one on the ground with the gun. I kicked that one. A lot. "Judah!" I heard as a jug whipped past my face, hitting the guard with the baton and exploding in what turned out to actually be bleach. "LEVI!" I kneeled and grabbed the gun hand before he could regain enough sense to point it, pushing the button to eject the magazine from it almost from memory as I kept my eyes on the other two. Bleach guy had his own problems. Apparently the two separate caustic chemicals in his eyes were having a reaction to each other and his anatomy. I jerked the gun arm down and used the gunman's finger to pull the trigger, shooting the coughing guard in the foot sending him tumbling to the floor. "JUDAH." my brother said. He sounded like he was hyperventilating. "Alright. Good job Levi, lets--" I stopped as my wrist froze. Arrested in thin air, the Amethyst that my mother had worn glinting in a light that I couldn't see. I looked over my shoulder at the doorway and almost shat myself. Another person stood in the doorway silhouetted from the high windows in the hallway. I could hear Levi talking but not actually hearing him as I locked eyed with the figure. He nodded from under long brown hair tied at the nape of his neck, pointed down the hallway to our right, and held up three fingers. He pointed back towards the left, and held up a closed fist. What the fuck The stranger nodded again and stepped out of the doorframe, my wrist freeing itself as soon as he was gone. "JUDAH NOW IS NOT THE TIME TO BE PLAYING GAMES YOU COULD HAVE--" Trust the gut I guess. "There's three more coming from the right, we need to leave. Hang left and go first." Levi opened his mouth to argue. "Not. Now. My element. Not yours. Go." I pushed Levi out of the closet and to the left. I heard the stairwell door to my right slam open and the shout of "Stop right there!" I didn't have time to fully frisk the guards before we made our exit, but one of these things was not like the others in terms of pepper spray cans. A fogger, either grabbed as a just in case or mistakenly was now my boon and their loss. I pulled the pin and rolled it down the hallway towards them, catching up swiftly with Levi. "Time to leave." I said as Levi tried his keycard to get into the lobby. The machine beeped, but didn't unlock the door. "Again?" Levi groaned, and slapped the badge against the reader. Beep, no lock. I sighed and eyed the door. Probably reinforced since they were trying to keep people in safe from outside and outside safe from them. "Levi..." I seethed through gritted teeth. He slapped the badge again. Red light. Friendly beep. Violet light. My eyebrow rose as the door popped open. We piled out into the lobby and shut the door again. "Oh no Amy!" I heard my brother cry as he squeaked over to her. My own blood pressure subsiding enough to hear how wet he'd gotten his loafers while we were escaping the fight. He went over to the registry desk and Amy laid half sprawled out of her chair, eyes closed. I could see the badge clipped to her scrubs shifting rhythmically, so she was breathing. Levi was kneeled next to her and looking frantic. "Levi, put her in the other hallway. She'll be protected from anyone coming in, so will the patients, but we can't do anything with her right now. I think you melted that guy’s eyeballs." Levi stared at me I stared at him and gestured towards the entrance hallway that I'd been escorted to. He sighed, and with all the careful resignation of an offended pallbearer, he gently tugged Amy towards the door, swiped it open on the first try and gently moved her inside. He started to try to make her comfortable until I barked at him to come on, which after a moment he did. The door to the exit hallway was beeping. Apparently the guards weren't having much better luck with the badge reader than we were. I half ran, half marched Levi out to the Bronco and told him to get in, slamming myself inside the driver's side and gunning the engine. I reached across my bleach soaked brother, popping the glove compartment and revealing my sig. Levi eyed it and me. "You just drive around with that?" "I usually hope that I'm just driving around with it instead of needing to have it out."
