## Approved Summary
Dr. Isaac Sands joins a research expedition to investigate Gwynporth, a rumored ancient druid settlement hidden in the North Carolina mountains. What begins as an academic dig turns dangerous fast when the team finds deliberate stonework, unnatural plantings, and a hollow oak marker inscribed in an old Latin-derived script. When Dr. Margaret Jones reads the inscription aloud, something in the land answers. The tree collapses, the ground gives way, and whatever was sleeping beneath the site wakes up.
