Emails

November 20, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 8 Scene 4 draft 528 words

I sat with that thought for a second that didn’t seem long enough when the computer speakers beeped a happy note, blossoming a notification from Dad’s email client on the desktop.

Ryan looked up from his phone and shifted forward in the chair. “Hey, isn’t that?”

The dialogue box indicated the email was from Corvid’s Nest Research. I clicked it to open the email, and froze.

It had been sent to my dad, but more than that it was CC’d with a trio of emails. levi.sands@gardencreekcounselling.org, Tsands@sandsresearch.com, and my own personal email. My eyes narrowed. One, Corven or someone employed by him was hunting for dad. That much was obvious, why email all of his kids outside of not expecting him to access his own emails. Second, Dad didn’t have that email for me. Corven appeared to though.

“Looking for you. Heard there was trouble at the trail. Don’t speak to police until one of our suits gets there. Respond back ASAP.”

Well then. Ryan snorted audibly. “Well, if we didn’t want to talk to him before, definitely would after that email.” Not incorrect. “The question I have is why send it to all us kids too. That seems… sloppy if you are trying to hide something. A lot of eyes.” “That is assuming he wanted it to not be seen.” Ryan said. “I think this is more a preemptive back off than an offer of legal assistance.” I frowned. Curious, I clicked into the inbox of the email client and looked in the sent folder. “Wait—” Ryan started reading along on the projector “Yep. That saved email phoned home. Someone back at the nest saw it accessed.” “And emailed Temperance and Levi too.” “What’s Garden Creek Counselling?” I asked, peering around the TV at my friend. Ryan started to open his mouth to explain, but the sound caught in his throat, he looked around perplexed, like he was trying to locate something. “Do you hear that?” I looked down at the desk. It was ringing? I opened the tray drawer, but there was nothing there. I could feel the vibrations in the wood from something. I started to close it back, but noticed a light on my jeans travel with it, and pawing the bottom of the tray discovered a small flip phone inside a pouch attached under the drawer. The small screen was lit with a number I didn’t recognize, and it wasn’t displaying a contact name What the hell dad. I looked up blankly at Ryan. “Well answer it?” He said, pulling out his phone and mouthing “on speaker.” I flipped open the phone and hit the green key. “May I assume I’m speaking to one of the Misters Sands?” I looked at Ryan who gave me a thumbs up. “You can assume that.” I said flatly. “Who am I speaking to?” “Well, based on the clipped tone, I’d assume that I’m speaking to the elder brother Judah.” the electronic and tinny speaker drolled. “My name is Kevin Corven, one of the founders of Corven Media, and we’d heard that your father had had an incident on his way back from an expedition we commissioned.”
