The Security Session

November 19, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 5 Scene 3 draft 590 words

It took a little bit, time I took walking Lee and Duilion through the photos and scene, and guiding them through what narrative I’d constructed with Ryan before they arrived. Lee seemed keen on an explanation of the heavily armed security, but I didn’t have an answer for that yet. “The only person that would know more about that would be my father, wherever he is, and the person that contracted the expedition. It’s certainly not normal for Dad to have this much security.”

Lee frowned, looking into the distance at nothing specific, deep in thought. We heard Chief Beech returning, more than we saw him coming through the darkness. Or more accurately we heard Cooperfield, who was still swearing a blue streak about the futility of all of this and how it was obviously an animal attack and there was nothing to see here. Cooperfield stopped short of our group on the trail, all gathered around a flashlight that we’d hooked the wrist cord around a branch to review the information we had. “Who the f-”

“Sorry, we already did that game and you missed it.” Ryan said dryly. But I noticed that he’d repositioned himself to the far side of the group from the approaching Beech and Cooperfield. “Sheriff, Chief, This is Agent Kassie Duilion and Agent Henry Lee from the SBI, we are just getting them up to speed on what we’ve gotten so far.”

I’d not thought that the sheriff could get much redder, but he took on a few more shades of purple upon hearing the SBI had arrived, that vein in his forehead engorging just a bit more, like a bloated earthworm under his skin.

“Agents,” He said, his voice a mock facade of pleasantry. “Welcome to the county, I’m sure you needn’t-”

“Sheriff.” Agent Lee said. Cutting the taller man off, who genuinely looked as if he’d no idea how to respond to that. “I’ve heard you are a straight shooter, so lets cut the crap. You’ve obstructed an SBI unit on state business, and have been doing nothing short of trying to get SBI to arrest you for years. We’re here now, there’s evidence that whatever these gentlemen were doing,” Lee indicated the photos he now held of the heavily armed men that had accompanied the expedition to wherever it was going. “Might be involved in one of our statewide wheelhouses, and not run of the mill county sheriff level crimes. So we aren’t going anywhere, and if you continue to obstruct, I may well remove your department from the chains of command and evidence entirely.”

The Sheriff turned from a beet red, to an ugly blotched purple, and I could hear a rumbling wail start to form at the base of his chest and rush up his throat, like a roaring inferno shooting up a mineshaft. Quickly, Beech stepped in. “Where I appreciate your frustration Agent- Lee was it? We should all take a breath and recall that there’s a pound of difference between what we are able to do, should do, and can do.” Beech being between them and sticking up for the sheriff, however mildly seemed to have knocked Cooperfield off balance, and the inferno extinguished. “That being said, you are already here, there’s no point in bickering the details of it.”

“But why are they here?” Cooperfield grunted, obviously not anymore pleased than he’d started, despite his temper cooling.

“Because I asked them to be. It was an awful lot of bodies.” the chief said with a smile.
