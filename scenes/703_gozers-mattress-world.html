Gozer's Mattress World

November 20, 2025 Act 1 Chapter 7 Scene 3 draft 617 words

I looked dumbly at the crack in the door, and back to the wall. i wanted to pinch myself, but that seemed a little reductive. There had to be something going on right? I looked at the floor again. No weird mood light refracting the light, no additional light sources in the hallway. I stepped over to Temperance’s door and leaned my head towards it. Nothing out of the ordinary. Rain noise, probably her phone. Pain. Wince. I hadn’t had a real run of tinnitus in a long time, much less anything this intense. I rocked back from the door. I noticed the burn in my shoulder for the first time. Like a drill had been pressed into the joint, and the friction from the bit was radiating heat into the muscles around it. “Temp?” I asked hoarsely, shaking my head and turning the knob to her bedroom door. I was bathed in the pink hued glow as it swung back into the room, revealing a sight I don’t think I’d ever unsee. Temperance was floating. Like, full on magician’s assistant, wave your hands under her and over the table level floating over the mattress. Her hair looked like she was under water, red curls splayed around her like a puddle of molten copper, reflecting the light and casting shadows across her face, where he green eyes almost glowed in the darkness. “Oh.” I stopped short. This doesn’t happen. Now I really did give my forearm a quick pinch. No change. Well, one could hope. I took a step further and winced again as the ringing in my ears got louder. No. Not ringing. It was almost a tune. Like if you pitched a Gregorian chant like nine billion octaves. I blinked through the pain, my eyes blurry with tears. I saw something in her left hand. A pulse of pink light. That matched the throb in my shoulder. Magic Stone. God damn it. I took another step forward, closing the gap between us. I felt repulsed by the stone. Like I didn’t want to be any where near it. But I also saw her arm. the veins coming down her palm, up her wrist like black tendrils, following up and disappearing into the sleeve of the oversized short sleeve shirt she was sleeping in. I mean, that’s not good right? Nothing good ever comes from blackened veins. I’ve seen plenty of horror movies. That’s the possession shit taking hold. Getting deep into the blood. I forced myself to close the gap, Where’d this thing even come from? Dad had drawn it in the notebook, but — Oh no. The hole in the bag in my pocket. I brought it. I was close enough to her to hear that she was whispering. Staring at the ceiling with affirmatively glowing green eyes and whispering. Cause this couldn’t get more Scooby-Doo meets the exorcist. It was gibberish. Or some weird demon speech. Except… Except it wasn’t. It was Welsh. And I didn’t know why I knew that. I froze. Everything about this was freaking me the fuck out. “Deffr, cysgadwr. Y mae’r amser i’r gair iachaid” she intoned, her words harsh like someone who had a severe case of laryengitus. It didn’t even sound like my sister-in-law’s voice at this point. “Yr ysbryd a groesodd dy lwybr, bellach ynghlymedig yw” Nope. Can’t deal with this. I lashed my arm out and really hoped I didn’t also get crazy Welsh demon possessed. got my fingers around the stone and ripped it from her grasp. and nothing happened. Well, to me. Temper fell out of the air and landed on her bed and screamed, which, admittedly, caused me to also scream.
