Book One: The Garnet Shield

The mountains keep their dead close.

Bear attack. That's what they said over the phone.

Retired Master-at-Arms Judah Sands came home to Deep Springs, North Carolina expecting a tragedy he could bury and then return to his own life. Instead, he found a dead research expedition, a story already being fought over by politicians, media men, and the faithful, and too many people with something to gain from keeping the truth buried.

At the center of it all was Dr. Isaac Sands.

His father had not died with the rest of the expedition.

He had not come home either.

What should have been a visit for closure was becoming an extended stay.

The premise

Folklore is not decoration. It is infrastructure.

Judah knows how to work a scene. He does not know why the massacre has left his father missing, why every lead circles back to a cairn that was never meant to be found, or what has been waiting in the ridgeline for the Sands family to come home. The closer he gets to the truth, the harder the mountain works to keep it.
