The premise

Some legends survive because somebody is still paying for them.

When Dr. Isaac Sands vanishes on a cryptozoological expedition, his son Judah is dragged back to the Smokies, where old legends die hard and the mountains keep their dead close.

A retired Navy investigator, Judah knows how to work a scene. What he does not know is why a local massacre has left his father missing, why the trail keeps circling a cairn that was never meant to be found, or what has been waiting in the ridgeline for the Sands family to come home.

Bloodline brings investigative grit into contemporary Appalachia, where folklore is not decoration. It is infrastructure.
